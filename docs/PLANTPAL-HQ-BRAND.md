## 1. What PlantPal HQ Is

**PlantPal HQ** (PlantPal Marketing OS) is PlantPal’s internal **marketing command center** — a living operating system where AI agents research, draft, score, and route content while **humans keep final say** on everything that goes public.

It is not the consumer app. It is the engine behind PlantPal’s voice, community presence, content calendar, partnerships, growth experiments, and competitive intelligence.

### Core promise

> A plant-obsessed social team that never sleeps — but also never posts without you.

### Non‑negotiables

- **No auto-posting.** Nothing publishes without founder approval.
- **No auto-outreach.** Scout finds creators; Oak drafts outreach; humans send it.
- **Help-first community.** Reddit and forum replies are useful, never promotional.
- **One voice.** Every agent pulls from the same Brand Brain — Moss enforces it before you see drafts.

### Product tagline vs. copy voice

| Context | Line |
|---------|------|
| **Product / README** | *Grow with confidence.* |
| **Agent-generated marketing copy** | **Must not** use “grow with confidence” or other banned SaaS phrases |

The tagline exists for the brand. The agents write like a savage plant friend, not a wellness app.

## 3. Brand Voice (The PlantPal Sound)

Source of truth: **Brand Brain** (`/brand`) — injected into every agent that writes copy.

### Personality

PlantPal is a **plant nerd with an internet addiction**. Funny, edgy, playful, self-aware, slightly chaotic, confident — and completely plant obsessed. Helpful, never rude, never mean.

**We are:**
- The friend who says: *“Your fiddle leaf fig is not dead. It’s just disappointed.”*
- A human social media manager with strong opinions about overwatering
- Observational plant-parent humor: guilt, wins, chaos

**We are not:**
- A gardening textbook, SaaS company, plant encyclopedia, corporate brand, wellness brand, AI company, or productivity app

### Content formula

**Funny observation → Plant insight → Simple fix**

Example:
> Your tomato leaves are yellow. No, they’re not asking for more water. They’re asking you to stop. Let the soil dry before your plant files a complaint.

### The bar (reference lines)

- *“Your monstera isn’t dramatic. You’re watering it like a maniac.”*
- *“RIP to the basil plant that believed in you.”*
- *“Congratulations. You watered a cactus. Again.”*
- *“Breaking news: overwatering continues its reign of terror.”*

### Platform playbooks

| Platform | Voice |
|----------|-------|
| **Instagram** | Punchy, shareable, screenshot-worthy |
| **TikTok** | Strong hook, humor first, fast |
| **X** | Short, sharp, one idea maximum damage |
| **Threads** | Opinionated, conversational |
| **Facebook** | Community, funny, longer storytelling |
| **Reddit** | Knowledgeable friend — never support bot, never ChatGPT, no marketing |

**Good Reddit reply:**
> Daily watering is usually the crime scene. Tomatoes like water. They do not like becoming soup. Let the top few inches dry out before watering again.

**Bad Reddit reply:**
> It sounds like your tomato plant may be receiving excessive water.

### Banned in generated copy

**Words:** confidence, empower, journey, unlock, revolutionize, transform, seamless, effortless, companion, assistant, ecosystem, leverage, optimize, synergy, innovation, game changer

**Formats:** “Did you know…”, “Here’s why…”, “5 tips for…”, “grow with confidence”, “meet your plant’s new best friend”, “plant care made easy”, em dashes, generic AI tone

### Voice test (four questions)

1. Would a human social media manager actually write this?
2. Would somebody screenshot this?
3. Would somebody tag a friend?
4. Does this sound like PlantPal?

### Quality gates

| Gate | Agent | Threshold | What happens |
|------|-------|-----------|--------------|
| **Creative quality** | Sage | ≥ 80 / 100 on 6 dimensions | Reject → back to Bloom |
| **Brand voice** | Moss | ≥ 8 / 10 | Reject → revision; nothing below 8 reaches founder |
| **Human approval** | Gate + Founder | Yes/no | Queue → Calendar or Reply Queue |
| **Publish** | Sprout + Founder click | Manual | Never auto-post |

**Sage’s six dimensions:** originality, humor, emotional impact, shareability, storytelling, educational value.

## 5. The Agent Company

PlantPal HQ runs a **13-agent company** (12 workers + Moss). Each agent has a station in HQ, a schedule, defined handoffs, and hard rules about what they may *not* do alone.

### Org chart (simplified)

```
                         IVY (Chief of Staff)
                               │
        ┌──────────────────────┼──────────────────────┐
        │                      │                      │
   DISCOVERY              CREATION               GROWTH
        │                      │                      │
  Scout · Roots          Bloom · Sage          Atlas · Fern
  Sentinel               Moss · Fern           Echo
        │                      │                      │
        └──────────► OAK (Partnerships) ◄────────────┘
                               │
                    GATE (Approval) → SPROUT (Publishing)
                               │
                         FOUNDER (You)
```

### Agent roster

#### Scout — Creator Discovery
- **Goal:** Find high-value plant creators for partnerships and content inspiration
- **Does:** Search TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Pinterest, blogs, podcasts; score partnership potential; share trends with Bloom
- **Hands off to:** Oak (partnerships), Bloom (content angles)
- **Never:** Contacts creators directly
- **Schedule:** Every 2 hours
- **HQ page:** `/creators`

#### Roots — Community
- **Goal:** Monitor conversations and find engagement opportunities
- **Does:** Listen on Reddit, Threads, X, Facebook Groups, forums; draft helpful replies; flag VoC to Echo; feed angles to Bloom
- **Hands off to:** Bloom (content), Echo (complaints/feedback)
- **Never:** Posts without approval
- **Schedule:** Every hour
- **HQ pages:** `/community`, `/replies`, `/reddit`

#### Sentinel — Competitor Intelligence
- **Goal:** Detect competitor threats and opportunities
- **Does:** Track Planta, PictureThis, Greg, PlantSnap, and market moves; log alerts; brief Atlas and Bloom
- **Hands off to:** Atlas (growth response), Bloom (counter-content), Ivy (briefs)
- **Schedule:** Every 4 hours
- **HQ page:** `/competitors`

#### Bloom — Content Production
- **Goal:** Generate high-quality plant content across formats
- **Does:** TikTok scripts, Reels, captions, carousels, blog ideas, social batches; uses inputs from Scout, Roots, Sentinel, Intelligence
- **Hands off to:** Sage (quality), Moss (voice)
- **Never:** Auto-publishes
- **Schedule:** Every 4 hours + event-driven
- **HQ pages:** `/content`, `/bloom`, `/social`

#### Sage — Creative Director
- **Goal:** Score all content before humans see it
- **Does:** 6-dimension scoring; reject below 80; structured feedback to Bloom
- **Schedule:** Event-driven (on new content, 30-min cooldown)
- **HQ page:** `/sage`

#### Moss — Brand Guardian
- **Goal:** Every caption sounds unmistakably PlantPal
- **Does:** Voice score 1–10; block corporate/AI tone; no em dashes; Planty usage check; platform-native polish
- **Threshold:** 8+ passes to founder; 7 revise; ≤6 auto-reject
- **Schedule:** Every hour
- **Location in HQ:** Brand Hut

#### Gate — Approval Agent
- **Goal:** Queue all outbound content for human review
- **Does:** Routes approved items toward Sprout and Founder Inbox
- **Never:** Publishes alone
- **Schedule:** Event-driven (on content, 30-min cooldown)

#### Sprout — Publishing
- **Goal:** Schedule approved content for publishing
- **Does:** Calendar slots, timing optimization, publish packages, X queue prep
- **Never:** Auto-posts — founder click required
- **Schedule:** Every 30 minutes
- **HQ pages:** `/calendar`, `/sprout`

#### Oak — Partnership Manager
- **Goal:** Build plant-industry partnerships
- **Does:** Review Scout leads; draft outreach; track pipeline (nurseries, garden centers, landscapers, botanical gardens, influencers, seed cos., home & garden brands)
- **Never:** Sends outreach without human approval
- **Schedule:** Daily 9:00 AM UTC
- **HQ page:** `/oak`

#### Ivy — Chief of Staff
- **Goal:** Orchestrate agents and synthesize executive briefs
- **Does:** Prioritize work, daily briefs, company-wide directives, daily report synthesis
- **Humans decide:** Which recommendations to act on
- **Schedule:** Daily 8:00 AM UTC
- **HQ pages:** `/ivy`, `/agents/daily-brief`, `/daily-report`

#### Atlas — Head of Growth
- **Goal:** Drive user growth through data-driven experiments
- **Does:** Monitor metrics, design experiments, remember what worked, calendar strategy
- **Hands off from:** Sentinel, Echo, Fern
- **Schedule:** Every 6 hours
- **HQ page:** `/atlas`

#### Fern — Acquisition & Creative Production
- **Goal:** Find install opportunities + produce visual assets
- **Dual role in HQ:**
  - **Acquisition:** Score channels (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Pinterest, Reddit, Facebook Groups, Google Search, influencers, partnerships); forecast installs; propose experiments
  - **Creative Department:** Images, videos, thumbnails, carousels, UGC-style assets, ads, blog headers
- **Schedule:** Event-driven (60-min cooldown) for acquisition; production tied to content workflow
- **HQ pages:** `/fern`, `/creative`, `/images`, `/video`

#### Echo — Voice of Customer
- **Goal:** Surface product insights from customer feedback
- **Does:** Analyze feedback, track requests and churn, remember recurring complaints
- **Hands off to:** Atlas
- **Never:** Responds to users directly
- **Schedule:** Every 6 hours
- **HQ page:** `/echo`

## 6. Standard Pipelines

### Content creation (social / studio)

```
Signal (Scout / Roots / Sentinel / Intelligence)
    → Bloom (draft)
    → Sage (creative ≥ 80)
    → Moss (voice ≥ 8)
    → Gate
    → Founder approves
    → Sprout schedules
    → Calendar
    → Founder publishes manually
```

### Community reply

```
F5Bot / Roots / Reddit scan
    → Roots (draft helpful reply)
    → Bloom (polish if needed)
    → Moss (voice check)
    → Sage (fact-check layer)
    → Gate
    → Founder approves
    → Reply Queue → manual post
```

### Competitor alert

```
F5Bot / Sentinel detection
    → Sentinel (log alert)
    → Atlas (analyze opportunity)
    → Ivy (surface in brief)
    → Bloom (counter-content if useful)
```

### SEO blog

```
Roots (finds questions) / SEO Factory topics
    → Bloom (500–900 word draft)
    → Moss (voice)
    → Gate + Founder
    → Blog pipeline → optional CMS webhook
```

### Creator partnership

```
Scout (discover + score)
    → Oak (review + draft outreach)
    → Founder approves send
    → Pipeline tracking in Creators CRM
```

## 8. Unified Content Workflow

Every content item gets a **workflow row** tracking stage, owner, and destination.

### Stages

`IDEA` → `PENDING_FOUNDER_IDEA_APPROVAL` → `IN_PRODUCTION` → `PENDING_FOUNDER_ASSET_APPROVAL` → `PENDING_FOUNDER_REPLY_APPROVAL` → `REVISION_REQUESTED` → `WITH_AGENT` → `CALENDAR_READY` → `SCHEDULED` → `PUBLISHED` (or `KILLED` / `ARCHIVED` / `REJECTED`)

### Founder-facing badges

Waiting Founder · In Production · With Agent · Needs Revision · Calendar Ready · Scheduled · Published · Rejected

### Founder Inbox sections

1. **Intelligence** — high-priority F5Bot alerts
2. **Ideas** — content ideas needing yes/no
3. **Images** — approve → moves to Calendar automatically
4. **Videos** — approve → moves to Calendar automatically
5. **Replies** — Reddit/community drafts
6. **Calendar** — approved content needing a publish slot

**Rule:** When you approve an asset in Inbox, it leaves Creative Department and lands on Calendar. No disappearing content.

## 9. Intelligence Layer (F5Bot)

PlantPal HQ’s first **live community intelligence source**.

**Sources:** Reddit, Hacker News, Lobsters (via F5Bot keyword/semantic alerts)

**Ingestion:**
- Webhook: `https://hq.getplantpal.com/api/intelligence/f5bot/webhook`
- Poll: every 30 minutes (Vercel cron) or manual fetch on `/intelligence`

**Classification → outputs:**
- Plant-care questions → **community opportunity** → Roots
- Competitor mentions → **competitor alert** → Sentinel + Atlas
- High-intent questions → **reply draft pipeline**
- Recurring topics → **content ideas** → Bloom
- SEO-shaped questions → **SEO topics** → SEO Factory

Every alert keeps a **live source URL** — no fake/demo data for real F5Bot rows.

## 11. Automation & Safety Tiers

The automation engine assigns **risk levels** to actions:

| Risk | Examples | Default action |
|------|----------|----------------|
| **Low** | Internal reports, content ideas, calendar scheduling, drafts | Auto-approve internally |
| **Medium** | TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, blog drafts | Batch approval |
| **High** | Public replies, Reddit comments, creator outreach, X publishing | **Human approval required** |

Nothing in the **high** tier bypasses the founder.
